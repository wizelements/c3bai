# AI Client Acquisition Prompt Pack
## Ready-to-use prompts for small food, retail, and service businesses

Copy each prompt, fill in the bracketed details, and run it in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI assistant.

## 1. Client Acquisition Strategy Prompt

**Goal:** Generate a complete 30-day client acquisition plan for a local business.

```
Act as a revenue strategist for small [industry] businesses.
My business: [business name]
What we sell: [products/services]
Ideal customer: [describe ideal customer]
Current monthly revenue: $[amount]
Goal in 90 days: $[amount]
Main marketing channel today: [e.g., Instagram, word of mouth, Google]
Biggest pain: [e.g., no online ordering, too many manual DMs, no follow-up]

Create a 30-day client acquisition plan that includes:
1. The single best lead magnet or offer to promote first.
2. The exact headline and subheadline for the landing page.
3. A 5-message follow-up sequence (email or SMS) for new leads.
4. Three free or low-cost traffic sources to test.
5. The one metric we should track weekly.
```

## 2. Follow-Up Message Sequence Prompt

**Goal:** Write a warm, non-pushy follow-up sequence that converts leads into bookings or sales.

```
Act as a sales copywriter for a small [industry] business.
Business: [business name]
Offer: [lead magnet or service]
Price: $[amount] or "book a free [audit/call]"
Tone: [friendly / professional / playful]

Write a 5-message follow-up sequence for a lead who just downloaded [lead magnet name].
- Message 1: Immediate delivery + set expectation.
- Message 2: Day 1 — ask one qualifying question.
- Message 3: Day 3 — share a short case study or result.
- Message 4: Day 7 — make a low-friction offer.
- Message 5: Day 14 — polite reactivation.

Each message must be under 120 words, have one clear goal, and include a single call to action.
```

## 3. Landing Page Copy Prompt

**Goal:** Get a complete landing page draft from a few inputs.

```
Act as a conversion copywriter.
Write a landing page for this offer:

Business: [business name]
Industry: [industry]
Offer name: [name of lead magnet or service]
What they get: [list 3–5 deliverables]
Desired outcome: [what the customer wants]
Biggest pain it removes: [pain point]
Social proof: [one testimonial or result]
CTA: [e.g., "Download the free playbook" or "Book your audit"]

Include:
1. A headline using the formula: "Get [outcome] in [timeframe] without [pain]."
2. A subheadline that explains how and who it is for.
3. Five bullet benefits (not features).
4. A short "what happens next" section.
5. A final CTA section.
```

## 4. Email Sequence Prompt

**Goal:** Build a welcome + nurture email sequence for new subscribers.

```
Act as an email marketer for a small [industry] business.
Business: [business name]
Lead magnet: [name]
Subscriber pain: [pain point]
Subscriber desired outcome: [outcome]

Write a 5-email welcome sequence:
1. Email 1 (immediate): Deliver the lead magnet and introduce the business.
2. Email 2 (day 2): Share the biggest mistake customers make before working with us.
3. Email 3 (day 4): Tell a short customer success story.
4. Email 4 (day 6): Address the top objection (price, time, trust).
5. Email 5 (day 8): Make a direct offer with a clear CTA.

Each email should be 150–250 words, friendly, and have one CTA.
```

## 5. Social Proof Collection Prompt

**Goal:** Turn customer feedback into high-converting testimonials and case studies.

```
Act as a marketing strategist.
I run [business name], a [industry] business.
I want to collect better testimonials and turn them into landing page social proof.

1. Write 5 specific questions I should ask every happy customer.
2. Draft a review-request message I can send automatically after a successful order/project.
3. Turn this rough customer feedback into a polished testimonial:

"[paste raw customer feedback here]"

4. Turn the same feedback into a 3-sentence mini case study with a before/after structure.
```
