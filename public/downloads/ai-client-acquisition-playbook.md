## 1. The Revenue System Map

A complete AI revenue system has five layers:

| Layer | Purpose | Example Tools |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| **Attract** | Get found locally and online | Google Business, Instagram, SEO, referrals |
| **Capture** | Turn visitors into contacts | Landing page, lead form, QR code, SMS opt-in |
| **Qualify** | Filter serious buyers from tire-kickers | AI chat, short questionnaire, calendar link |
| **Convert** | Take payment or book the appointment | Square, Stripe, Calendly, Acuity |
| **Retain** | Follow up, reactivate, and get reviews | Email sequence, Telegram alerts, review requests |

You do not need all five on day one. Start with **Capture + Convert**, then add the rest.

## 2. The Lead-Capture Page Template

Every lead magnet, service, or offer needs its own landing page with:

- **One clear headline** — the outcome the visitor wants.
- **One subheadline** — how you deliver it and who it is for.
- **3–5 bullet benefits** — not features, benefits.
- **One email capture form** — name + email minimum.
- **One primary CTA** — what they get after submitting.
- **Social proof** — testimonial, case study, or trust badge.
- **No navigation distractions** — hide the main menu if possible.

### Example Headline Formula

> “Get [desired outcome] in [timeframe] without [biggest pain].”

For a local bakery:

> “Get 20+ pre-orders per week without spending hours on Instagram DMs.”

## 3. The AI Follow-Up Sequence

Most leads do not buy on first contact. Use this 5-message sequence:

1. **Immediate** — deliver the lead magnet + set expectation.
2. **Day 1** — ask one qualifying question.
3. **Day 3** — share a relevant case study or result.
4. **Day 7** — make a low-friction offer (audit, sample, call).
5. **Day 14** — reactivation: “Still looking for help with [outcome]?”

Keep each message under 120 words. One goal per message.

## 4. Social Proof Collection System

Before-and-after proof converts better than any copy. Collect it automatically:

- After a successful order or project, send a review request.
- Ask for a specific metric: “How many new leads did you get this month?”
- Offer a small incentive: 10% off next order, entry into a giveaway.
- Display the best proof on your landing page as a testimonial card.

## 5. Tool Stack for Small Businesses

| Job | Recommended Tool | Why |
|-----|------------------|-----|
| Website / landing pages | Next.js + Vercel | Fast, SEO-friendly, easy to scale |
| Database | Turso (libSQL) | Serverless SQLite, low cost |
| Email | Resend | Reliable, developer-friendly |
| Payments | Square or Stripe | Local businesses often already use Square |
| Alerts | Telegram bot | Free, instant, no SMS fees |
| Scheduling | Calendly or Acuity | Removes back-and-forth |
| Forms + validation | React + Zod | Type-safe, clean UX |

## 6. Metrics That Matter

Track these weekly:

- **Visitors** — how many people saw your offer.
- **Capture rate** — % of visitors who gave their email.
- **Qualified leads** — % of captures who fit your ideal customer.
- **Conversion rate** — % of qualified leads who paid or booked.
- **Revenue per lead** — total revenue ÷ total leads.
- **Cost per lead** — ad or time spend ÷ total leads.

If you only track one number, track **revenue per lead**.

## 7. The 30-Day Launch Plan

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Pick one offer, write the landing page copy, set up the form. |
| 2 | Connect email confirmation, build the lead magnet, test end-to-end. |
| 3 | Drive traffic: social posts, QR code, Google Business update, email list. |
| 4 | Review metrics, fix leaks, add follow-up messages. |

Do not try to perfect everything before launch. Launch small, measure, improve.
