## Taste of Gratitude — Wellness Beverage E-Commerce

### The Problem
A wellness beverage brand was selling at local markets and taking manual orders via DMs and spreadsheets. They needed:
- Online weekly menu with rotating inventory
- E-commerce checkout for pickup and local delivery
- Order alerts to the kitchen team
- Market funnel that captured leads at events

### The Solution
- **Type:** Next.js PWA + Stripe/Square + SMS alerts
- **Timeline:** 6 weeks
- **Systems installed:** E-commerce, weekly menu, order alerts, market funnel

### Results
- Orders moved from DMs to automated checkout
- Kitchen gets instant SMS/email alerts for each order
- Market funnel captures leads and follows up automatically

## What We Learned

Every project taught the same lesson: the highest ROI is not a prettier website. It is removing manual work from the business owner.

The systems that pay for themselves fastest are:
1. Lead capture + automatic follow-up
2. Booking/payment without phone calls
3. Admin dashboards that let staff self-serve
4. Alerts and reports that surface what matters

That is why Cod3Black Agency focuses on business systems, not just websites.
